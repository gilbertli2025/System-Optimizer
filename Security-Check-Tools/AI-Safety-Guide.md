# How to Use AI to Stay Safe Online (for normal users)

AI assistants (Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini) can genuinely help you spot and
avoid cyber threats — **if used as a helper, not as a replacement** for your
antivirus, updates, MFA, and backups.

---

## What AI is good at (use it for these)

- **Phishing checks** — paste a suspicious email or link and ask:
  *"Is this a scam? What would a scammer say here?"*
- **Explaining warnings** — ask it to decode a Windows/antivirus message or a
  fake "Microsoft support" phone call script.
- **Guiding recovery** — "I think I was hacked, what do I do first?"
- **Spotting social engineering** — AI is good at detecting urgent-pressure,
  "act now" language and fake invoices/password-reset requests.

---

## What NOT to do (important)

- **Never paste passwords, credit cards, or recovery codes** into any AI.
- **Do not give an AI agent admin access** or let it read your email/browser
  by default. A powerful AI with broad access becomes an attack target
  (prompt injection: hidden text in a webpage/email can trick the AI).
- **Do not rely on AI alone** — it can be wrong. For serious action (a real
  infection), use a scanner and follow Windows/account steps.

---

## A safe "ask the AI" checklist

1. Forward a **suspicious email** (sender, subject, link, urgency) → ask if it
   looks like a scam.
2. Before clicking a **link**, ask the AI to explain what the real site's URL
   should look like.
3. If you get a **"your account is locked"** popup → ask the AI whether
   Microsoft really contacts users that way (it doesn't).
4. For **unexpected downloads** → ask the AI if the software/domain is known
   or suspicious before running it.
5. To **verify a process** you don't recognize → copy the process name from
   Task Manager and ask the AI what it is.

---

## The bottom line

- Keep **core defenses on**: antivirus, Windows updates, MFA, a standard daily
  account, backups.
- Use AI as an **advisory helper** — it's great at thinking *with* you and
  catching tricks, but it should not have the keys to your PC or accounts.
- The safest "AI" protecting you today is the machine-learning inside your
  antivirus, plus your own caution.
